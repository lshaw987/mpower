Control your interruptability

The Great Switch Off

You don't have a time problem. You have an interruptability problem. Every ping costs you around 23 minutes to reclaim your focus, restart the thinking and rebuild the depth - and it's never quite the same. Start small, with your notifications, and work outward. Tick these off over a week and write down what you changed.

Your devices

Turn off every non-essential notification, on every device. If it doesn't need immediate action, it doesn't need to interrupt you.

Remove badges and pop-ups. Those red dots and pings are designed to pull you in.
