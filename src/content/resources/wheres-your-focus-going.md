Control what you can. Let go of what you can't. Sounds simple, right?

And yet so much energy gets wasted worrying about things completely outside our control - what other people think, world events, the latest policy change, whether it's going to rain on Saturday.

Here's a better way to think about it. Three circles, one inside the other. It builds on Stephen Covey's circles of concern and influence.

🟢 **Circle of control.** The things you have direct power over - your decisions, your actions, your mindset. This is where real progress happens.

🔵 **Circle of influence.** The things you can shape indirectly - the culture around you, your relationships, how a project lands. Influence is built through trust and action, over time.

🟣 **Circle of concern.** The things you have zero control over - the economy, other people's opinions, global trends. The more time you spend here, the more drained you feel.

A small shift in focus changes a lot. What if, instead of getting caught in the concern spiral, you put your energy where it actually makes a difference?

So, a challenge. Grab a piece of paper, draw three circles, and start placing what's on your mind into the right one. The shift in perspective is real, and it happens on the page in front of you.

People who use this well tend to find they get more done, because the effort goes where it counts. They feel less stressed, because they've stopped burning energy on what they can't change. And they make better decisions, because they're focused where their actions actually land.

So, where's your energy going today? Is it helping you move forward, or keeping you stuck?
